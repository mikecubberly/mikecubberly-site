Hello.
I'm Mike.

Most growth doesn't come from clever ideas. It comes from systems that capture signal, sustain rhythm, and convert intent. I build those systems for founders and revenue leaders who would rather move quietly and arrive first.

Today I run Bottle Rocket Growth, a Chicago-based LinkedIn outbound agency for B2B startups, and Bottle Rocket Racing, the firm's motorsports business development arm partnered with elite race teams including in NASCAR. Before founding the Bottle Rocket family of brands in 2024, I spent a decade building go-to-market machinery at Verkada, Glencoco, Primer, and RevGenius.

The thread through all of it is the same. Find the signal. Build the system. Let the work compound.
