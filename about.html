About

Founder, builder, perpetual operator.

By Mike Cubberly • Published June 2024 • Last updated April 29, 2026 • Chicago, Illinois

Mike Cubberly is the founder of Bottle Rocket Growth, a Chicago-based LinkedIn outbound agency and go-to-market consultancy, and Bottle Rocket Racing, the firm's motorsports business development arm. Mike has spent over a decade building go-to-market systems for B2B technology companies, with a particular focus on the messy, operational layer where outbound, content, community, and partnerships compound into pipeline.

Mike lives in Chicago and works with founders and revenue leaders across SaaS, infrastructure, fintech, and motorsports.
