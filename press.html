Boilerplate

Mike Cubberly is the founder of Bottle Rocket Growth, a Chicago-based LinkedIn outbound agency and go-to-market consultancy, and Bottle Rocket Racing, the firm's motorsports business development arm. Mike has spent over a decade building go-to-market systems for B2B technology companies, with prior roles at Verkada, Glencoco, Primer, and RevGenius, where he continues to serve as Community Admin. He launched Bottle Rocket Growth and Bottle Rocket Racing in 2024 and is based in Chicago, Illinois.

Mike speaks on go-to-market and operator-led growth. Common themes include LinkedIn outbound at scale, signal-based prospecting, the borrowed sender profile model, motorsports business development, and how community fits into modern GTM motions.
